      Good to know

      Shared license apps have a limited number of seats that are shared across campus. If one won't open because all seats are in use, close it, wait a few minutes, and try again, or try another room.

      Some rooms are used for scheduled classes, so check the posted schedule before settling in.

      Can't find what you need, or something isn't working? Contact the IT Help Desk and let them know the room and the app.
